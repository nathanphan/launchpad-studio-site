Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Launchpad Studio (the "App") is a macOS application provided by Nathan Phan. This policy explains what information the App handles and how. In short: the App runs on your Mac, we do not run servers that collect your data, and we do not sell or share your information.

1. Information the App handles

  • Videos and post content you select or type (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, schedules). These stay on your Mac and are sent only to the Platform you choose when you post.
  • Platform account information returned when you connect an account, such as display name, avatar, account or channel ID, and, for TikTok, the creator info used to show your nickname and allowed privacy options.
  • Access tokens issued by each Platform after you sign in. They are stored only in your macOS Keychain.
  • Developer credentials you enter in Settings (for example API keys), stored only in your macOS Keychain.

2. How the information is used

Only to provide the App's features: showing which account you are posting to, uploading and publishing the videos you choose, scheduling posts and showing their status. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. TikTok data

With your permission, the App uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. TikTok data (your basic profile information and tokens) is used only to post the videos you choose to your own TikTok account and to show you which account you are using. It is kept on your device, never sent to us, and never shared with or sold to third parties.

4. Where data goes

The App communicates directly between your Mac and the Platforms you connect (Google/YouTube, Meta/Facebook/Instagram, TikTok). Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive copies.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Remove an account in the App (Channels → Remove) to delete its token from your Keychain.
  • Deleting the App and its data folder removes all locally stored post history.
  • You can also revoke the App's access in each Platform's settings, for example TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the Platforms you use).

7. Security

Tokens and credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain and all communication with Platforms uses HTTPS.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.
