Publish your videos everywhere, from your Mac.

Launchpad Studio is a desktop app for creators. Pick a video on your computer, write the title and caption once, tailor them per platform, and post now or schedule it — to your own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Your accounts only

You sign in with each platform's official login and post only to accounts you own or manage.

You stay in control

Choose privacy, comments, Duet/Stitch and disclosures for every post. Nothing is posted without your action.

Private by design

No servers and no account with us. Sign-in tokens stay in your Mac's Keychain.

Schedule & track

Schedule posts, follow upload progress and see what was published where.

How it works with TikTok

Launchpad Studio uses TikTok Login Kit so you can connect your TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to upload videos you choose — either directly to your profile with the privacy setting you pick, or to your TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app.
